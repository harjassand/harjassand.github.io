An example of a non-sofic hyperbolic group

Harjas Sandhu and Andreas Thom

Manuscript dated 7 October 2026.
Posted on arXiv 17 October 2026 · Version 1

Read PDF 488 kB arXiv record DOI Cite

Abstract

We construct a torsion-free, two-dimensional, word-hyperbolic group which is not sofic. Our construction builds on OpenAI's construction of a non-residually finite hyperbolic group, see OpenAI 252.

Abstract reproduced from the arXiv record.

Contribution and provenance

This paper is based on an AI-generated construction obtained by Harjas Sandhu on October 7, 2026, which was in turn based on the OpenAI breakthrough [14]. The second author worked through the proof to make it readable and to reduce unnecessary overhead.

The construction inherits marked lines, triangular relations, an interpolation identity and comparisons of real and finite-field ranks from OpenAI 252. The acknowledgments also disclose AI assistance in drafting and explain how the paper relates these ingredients to earlier mathematics. The PDF hosted here is the unchanged arXiv v1 preprint.

The quotation is an excerpt from the paper’s acknowledgments. “The second author” refers to Andreas Thom. Read the full paper and acknowledgments.
