Complete information orders of quantum channels: equivalence and undecidability

Several ways to ask whether one quantum channel preserves more information turn out to give the same answer when every finite quantum reference is allowed. That complete comparison remains undecidable, even for strictly positive rational measurements.

The result

Quantum channels can be compared by their preservation of relative entropy, Holevo information or symmetric logarithmic derivative Fisher information. Allowing an arbitrary finite untouched quantum reference makes these three comparisons equivalent. Passing to complementary channels reverses the order.

The resulting comparison problem is coRE-complete, already for measurements with strictly positive rational-complex effects. The paper also gives an additive bounded entropy defect whose value cannot be computed to any fixed positive rational additive accuracy.

How the argument works

An untouched reference is an auxiliary quantum system that may be correlated with the input. Its freedom connects local Fisher-information tests to global entropy comparisons through coherent blocks and resolvent identities. A purification identity relates information retained by the receiver to information lost to the environment.

For the undecidability reduction, finite nonlocal games are encoded into inverse Sylvester kernels and then into strictly positive measurement effects. Finite witnesses detect failed comparisons, but their required reference dimensions have no computable uniform bound.

Scope

Each individual channel, measurement and test is finite dimensional. Completeness quantifies over every finite reference dimension, with no common bound. The equivalence is a statistical information order; it does not by itself assert that one channel can be obtained by physically processing the other.

The fixed-accuracy obstruction concerns the bounded entropy defect defined in the paper. Its conclusion depends on the complete comparison and the specified exact input representation.

Technical abstract

With arbitrary finite quantum references, relative entropy, Holevo information and SLD Fisher information induce the same channel order, reversed by complementation. This order is coRE-complete, even for strictly positive rational POVMs. Its bounded entropy defect is additive and uncomputable at every fixed positive additive accuracy.
