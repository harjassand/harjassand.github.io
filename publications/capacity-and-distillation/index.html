Preprint · Quantum information

From complete channel order to capacity and one-way distillation

Manuscript contact: Harjas Sandhu

Abstract

A flagged channel with a four-dimensional auxiliary input turns every failed complete information comparison into positive private and quantum capacity. This transfers complete-order undecidability to exact-zero capacity and one-way distillation, even under any prescribed two-way distillation lower bound. All reference transmission and activation use the constructed channel itself.
