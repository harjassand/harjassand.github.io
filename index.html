Mathematics Student | AI & Scientific Discovery

I'm studying mathematics at the University of Queensland, majoring in Mathematical AI. I'm interested in how AI could help us solve difficult problems in mathematics and science, particularly those that are beyond what current methods can achieve.

I developed and coordinated an agentic AI system for independent investigations in mathematics and the physical sciences. I'm interested in how these systems can support reliable scientific research and build on previous discoveries.

Research interests

My main interests are machine learning and AI for scientific discovery. I'm particularly interested in applying advances in AI and computation to problems in physics, energy, and materials science.

I'm also interested in what it would take for AI systems to carry out reliable scientific research: developing new ideas, testing them, recognising mistakes, and building on previous discoveries.
