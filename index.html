  1. Global Readouts of Noisy Quantum DynamicsOpen quantum systems, classical simulation and complexity · 8 November 2026

    Independent local noise permits classical prediction of global product readouts in a driven two-dimensional quantum system. The algorithm has polynomial bit complexity at every fixed positive noise ratio, including full-rank biased resets when the Hamiltonian preserves their product reference.

    Harjas Sandhu · Preprint

  2. Quadratic Repetition for Commuting Projection Games and Noisy Quantum CommunicationQuantum information, complexity and operator algebras · 7 November 2026

    Repeating a projection game suppresses joint commuting strategies at a quadratic rate in the single-game gap. The paper proves this bound through exact local reconstruction and shows how amplified soundness can survive a precisely charged budget of interactive noisy quantum communication.

    Harjas Sandhu · Preprint

    Paper details PDF DOI
  3. Quantum Memory from Rare Thermal BoundariesQuantum information and mathematical physics · 6 November 2026

    A thermally rare error boundary can protect an unknown quantum state under exact system–bath dynamics. The paper proves storage bounds proportional to the boundary probability and holding time, and identifies the preparation and access assumptions needed to turn equilibrium estimates into a memory guarantee.

    Harjas Sandhu · Preprint

    Paper details PDF DOI
  4. Greatest Quantum Channels with Prescribed Maximal InformationQuantum information and operator algebras · 6 November 2026

    Quantum channels can share every complete maximal-information test while differing in what they can physically simulate. The paper constructs their greatest finite realizations and shows how atomicity and injectivity govern exact and approximate realizations for infinite outputs with a tracial invariant.

    Harjas Sandhu · Preprint

    Paper details PDF DOI
  5. Entropy Budgets and Weighted Concentration in Parallel GamesProbability and quantum information · 6 November 2026

    An explicit conditional entropy budget forces weighted concentration even when test outcomes are arbitrarily correlated. The paper also identifies barriers to positive-factor reconstruction; applications to commuting-operator games retain a separately stated reconstruction hypothesis.

    Harjas Sandhu · Preprint

  6. Finite Certificates and Undecidable Gibbs UniquenessMathematical physics · 6 November 2026

    At one fixed positive temperature, a family of classical spin models encodes whether a computation ever halts. Uniqueness is undecidable within this family, yet each model on its unique-equilibrium branch has exponentially decaying correlations.

About

My interests include machine learning, mathematical physics and the use of AI in scientific discovery. I develop and coordinate AI research workflows that explore ideas, test arguments and build on earlier results.

The papers collected here cover quantum information, the limits of computation and structural questions in mathematics. The group-theory preprint is joint work with Andreas Thom; individual paper pages retain their published authorship and contact roles.
