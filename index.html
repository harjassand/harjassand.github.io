Harjas Sandhu

Mathematics undergraduate | AI for scientific discovery & autonomous research

I am a Bachelor of Mathematics student at the University of Queensland, majoring in Mathematical AI. I investigate how AI systems can become capable scientific researchers, with a focus on autonomous discovery, mathematical reasoning, and the accumulation of verifiable knowledge.

I develop and direct an independent AI-assisted research project exploring whether autonomous systems can conduct original research that builds on prior results. My work involves designing research processes, coordinating independent AI investigations, and evaluating their outputs.

Research interests

My interests span AI for mathematics, theoretical machine learning, computational physics, and AI-driven discovery in the physical sciences. I am particularly interested in how mathematical reasoning, scientific computation, and accumulated knowledge can support autonomous research.

My broader aim is to develop research systems that deepen fundamental scientific understanding and help enable advances in science and technology.
